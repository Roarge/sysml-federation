three subgraphs, one merged graph, and what the services agree on

Who owns what

No service resolves a field another service resolves. The capacity service adds four fields to Part and Requirement, the document service two to Requirement, and every other field on those types is the adapter's. The entity types are Part, Requirement and VerificationCase, keyed on id, the SysML short name or, failing that, the qualified name (SR-21, AD-0018).

What the capacity service agrees to

It carries two configured names, capacity and throughput, and no other word of the model (SR-31). The rest of the agreement is the entity key and the field set its @requires declares, which the router fills from the adapter's answer and hands over as the representation. Its four fields are computed on read from the fields its @requires names (SR-28 to SR-32).

The first spike

Whether Cosmo composition and gqlgen accept a nested-list @requires, and where @external and @shareable go on the value types inside it, is the first implementation spike (C-15). The fallback keeps the same semantics behind one scalar: the adapter adds Part.wiring, a JSON document of the children and connections, and the capacity service requires subject { id name wiring } in place of the nested lists.
